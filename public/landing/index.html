What the agent actually does

01

It tests competing answers, not just one

Ask: “Does metformin lower cancer risk?”
It just reflects patient weight ruled out
It slows tumours via AMPK survives ✓
It only lowers blood glucose ruled out

Three explanations compete; evidence rules out the weak ones and keeps the survivor.

02

It searches 42 sources, not just papers

For that question, it pulled:
PubMed · 14 trials Europe PMC · 9 cohorts ChEMBL · metformin UniProt · AMPK Reactome · pathway + 37 more

03

It checks its own work

“risk cut 37%” — no source backs it
⚠ wrong citation

A blind reviewer catches these before you ever see the answer.

04

Frequently asked

What can it research?

Any field — medicine, biology, chemistry, genomics, ML, physics, economics. It searches 42 scientific databases across 7 domains and reasons over what it finds.

How is it different from a chatbot or search?

It doesn’t just retrieve. It forms competing hypotheses, designs experiments to tell them apart, runs real analysis, and returns a cited answer — the way a researcher would.

Does it make things up?

No. Every claim traces to a real source, and an independent reviewer flags untraceable numbers and citation mismatches before you ever see the answer. Zero fabricated data.

Can it run real analysis and code?

Yes. It writes and runs statistics, simulations, and data pipelines in a sandbox, and produces publication-quality figures — you get the plan and the exact code that ran.

Do I stay in control?

Always. Review the plan before it runs, steer it mid-course, and cancel anytime. Runs happen in the background, so you can close the tab and come back to the result.
