# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity. Participation here — issues, pull requests, discussions, or anything else — is expected to reflect that.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in disagreements; critique ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith, and give newcomers room to ask questions that might seem basic.
- Keep feedback on code and design focused and actionable.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments directed at another contributor.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests, and any other official communication channels — and to anyone representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening an issue marked confidential or by contacting the maintainer directly. All reports will be reviewed and addressed as promptly as possible. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or contributions that don't align with this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

## Attribution

This document is original to this project and intentionally short — the goal is a clear, enforceable baseline, not an exhaustive policy.
