Finding papers is only half the job. Reagent runs a **PRISMA-style systematic review**: it screens candidates against explicit criteria, extracts what each paper actually shows, and synthesizes the results by theme — flagging where the literature agrees, where it's still emerging, and where it contradicts itself. A review specialist runs this for each facet of a topic in parallel.

## The review flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identification">
    Cast a wide net across databases, then de-duplicate by DOI and title.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Screening">
    Define inclusion and exclusion criteria first, then judge each candidate include or exclude with a one-line rationale that cites a criterion.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Eligibility">
    For each included paper, extract the key claims (with numbers), the methods, and the authors' stated limitations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Synthesis">
    Group findings by theme — not paper by paper — separating consensus (three or more papers) from emerging (one or two recent) from contested.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify">
    Confirm every citation's authors, year, venue, and title before it's used.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Evidence quality

Each included paper is graded so strong multi-paper evidence isn't confused with a single weak study:

<CardGroup cols={4}>
  <Card title="Strong" icon="shield-check">Robust, replicated, well-powered.</Card>
  <Card title="Moderate" icon="shield">Solid but with caveats.</Card>
  <Card title="Preliminary" icon="flask-conical">Early or single-study.</Card>
  <Card title="Weak" icon="triangle-alert">Underpowered or contested.</Card>
</CardGroup>

## Contradictions are surfaced, not smoothed over

Reagent explicitly flags disagreements — *"Paper A reports X, Paper B reports Y, likely because…"* — instead of averaging them away. A contradiction is often where the interesting hypothesis lives.

<Panel title="Zero fabricated citations">
  Every factual claim carries a citation to a real, verified paper. Anything that can't be traced to a retrieved source is marked as needing a citation — never invented — and is caught again in the final [blind review](/workflow/findings).
</Panel>
