When an experiment resolves, Reagent applies the rule it committed to in advance and updates the board: rivals the result refutes are marked **refuted**, ones it supports are marked **supported**, each logged with the deciding evidence. The outcome also feeds back into the [tournament](/ranking/elo), so the ranking reflects real evidence — not just debate.

## The living documents

A research keeps a set of documents current as it works — they are Reagent's memory and your deliverables, and they persist across sessions:

## Synthesis

Reagent interprets the winning hypothesis against the literature and its pre-registered success criteria, names the limitations, confounders, and alternative explanations, and cites every substantive claim to a specific paper.

## A blind adversarial review

For a high-stakes answer, a **reviewer specialist** audits the finished output blind — hunting for claims the evidence doesn't support, numbers that trace to no source, and citations that don't match what they're attached to. It defaults to skepticism: a claim is guilty until the evidence proves it.

<Panel title="The write-up">
  When you need a paper, report, or review, a writing specialist turns the verified findings, figures, and citations into a clean, well-structured document — you provide the science, it provides the prose.
</Panel>

## Where it converges

The research is done when one hypothesis has survived its crucial test and meets its pre-registered criteria. If the budget runs out first, Reagent reports what leads and what's still open — honestly — rather than forcing a conclusion.
